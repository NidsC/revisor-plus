## 3. What the Service is

RevisorPlus provides 11+ practice questions, mock papers, a vocabulary trainer, progress
tracking against a target school and exam date, and tools for a linked tutor to set
homework. It is a practice aid. It is not tuition, it does not guarantee any exam result or
school place, and its questions are our own, not past papers of any exam board.

## 4. Free use and Premium

**Free use.** Anyone with an account can use the free resources: each pupil may answer up to
100 practice questions in each of the four papers (English, Maths, Verbal Reasoning,
Non-Verbal Reasoning) without paying. This is so you can see what the Service does. There is
no free trial of Premium.

**Premium.** Full access, including mock papers and the focus-areas analytics, requires a
subscription: **£29.99 per month, per pupil**, paid by the parent. Each pupil needs their
own subscription. Prices include VAT where applicable **[CHECK: VAT position of the
partnership]**.

We may change the price for future periods with at least 30 days' notice by email; a change
never applies to a period you have already paid for.

## 5. Paying, renewing and cancelling

- Subscriptions are monthly and renew automatically on the same date each month until
  cancelled. Payment is taken by Stripe from the card you give Stripe.
- You can cancel at any time from the **Manage billing** link on your family page, which opens
  Stripe's billing portal. Cancellation takes effect at the **end of the current paid month**:
  the pupil keeps Premium until then, nothing further is charged, and you can undo the
  cancellation from the same portal before the month ends.
- If a payment fails, Stripe will retry it for a short period and the pupil keeps Premium
  during that time. If it still fails, the subscription ends and the account returns to free
  use. Nothing is deleted.
- You can update your card through the same billing portal.

## 6. Your right to cancel and our refund policy

**Statutory cooling-off.** Because you buy online, the Consumer Contracts (Information,
Cancellation and Additional Charges) Regulations 2013 give you 14 days from the day you
subscribe to cancel the contract for any reason. Premium starts immediately when you pay, and
by subscribing you ask us to start it straight away.

**Our refund policy, which is more generous than the minimum:** if this is the **first time**
you have subscribed for this pupil and you ask within **14 days** of your first payment, we
refund that payment **in full**, whether or not Premium has been used. Email
revisorplus@gmail.com from your account address; we refund to the original card through
Stripe within 14 days of your request.

After those 14 days, and on any later renewal, there is no refund for an unused part of a
month, because you can cancel at any time and keep access to the end of what you have paid
for. Nothing in this section limits your rights if the Service is faulty (section 9).

account, or their own account (which deletes every pupil under it), at any time by emailing
us. See the Privacy Policy, sections 8 and 9.
