## What this is

RevisorPlus is being tested by a small number of invited families before it opens to the
public. It is free during the test. This page says who we are, what we collect, why, and
what you can ask us to do.

## Who we are

RevisorPlus is run by **[trading name and name of the sole trader]**. You can reach us at
**revisorplus@gmail.com**. We decide how the information below is used, so we are the
"controller" for it under UK data protection law.

## What we collect, and why

**From you, the parent or tutor:** your email address and a password, or the name and email
address Google gives us if you sign in with Google. We use these to run your account and to
reply to you. We do not send marketing emails.

**About your child:** the nickname you choose, their username and password, and, if you set
them, a target school, exam date and target scores. We ask for a nickname, not a full name.
We never ask for a child's age, date of birth, address, current school or photograph.

**From practice:** every question answered, whether it was right, how long it took, mock-paper
results, vocabulary-game progress, homework set by a parent or tutor, and messages between a
parent and a linked tutor. This is the information the site exists to show you: where your
child is doing well and what to practise next.

**Automatically:** a cookie that keeps you signed in, and the usual web-server logs, which
include your internet address. We do not use analytics or advertising trackers. The site's
design files are loaded from a public content network, which sees your internet address when
a page loads.

## Who else handles it

- **Render** hosts the website and its database in the United States, under its data
  processing terms and the UK extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.
- **Google**, only if you choose "Sign in with Google".
- **Stripe** handles payments. No payments are taken during the test; if a Subscribe button
  appears, please ignore it.

We do not sell or share this information with anyone else.

## How long we keep it

For the length of the test. When the test ends we will email you to say whether your
accounts are being deleted or carried over into the full service, and you can choose either
way. At any time you can ask us to delete your child's account, or your whole family's, and
we will do it within a month.

## Your rights

You can ask to see the information we hold about you or your child, have it corrected, or
have it deleted. Email **revisorplus@gmail.com**. If you are unhappy with how we handle it,
you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

## Test conditions

- The service is free during the test and may change or be unavailable without notice.
- It is a practice aid. It does not guarantee any exam result or school place.
- The questions and papers belong to RevisorPlus and are for your child's own practice.
- Keep logins private and do not share a child's login between children.
- Please tell us about anything that does not work. That is what the test is for.
