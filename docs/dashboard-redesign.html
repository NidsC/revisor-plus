Notes

What was cut, and why.

  • readiness_pct ("77% towards your target") - contains no coverage, no volume and no time; it is average accuracy over only the sections that happen to have data. Gone, not renamed.
  • correct_answers (490) - it is 66% of 746 restated. One of the two goes.
  • overall_accuracy (66%) - replaced by per-subject bands. No single accuracy figure on the page.
  • weekly_avg ("100% avg this week") - five attempts is not a percentage.
  • "Your wins" - every line in it restated something above it.
  • "Not enough data yet" on three of four subjects - replaced by a greyed row.

What stayed. Days remaining, one primary action, one merged task list, four subject rows.

Coverage is kept, on the rows. An earlier draft of this file cut pct_complete and "5 of 1404 questions" as inventory rather than information. That was reversed: each subject row now carries a chip, a coverage bar and an attempted-of-bank count, and the header standfirst drops its count so the figure appears once per page rather than twice in two different units. The contradiction the earlier draft was avoiding - an attempt count beside a distinct-answerable count - is avoided by there being only one count on the page, not by removing coverage.

The counts are distinct answerable questions attempted, over the answerable bank. They are much smaller than a pupil's attempt total, because a pupil answers the same question more than once: 3,530 attempts can read as "5 of 966 attempted". That is not a bug, but it is the number a parent will query, and it is worth a line of copy somewhere on the page.

Decisions you still need to make

  • Band wording. Shown here as Strong / Getting there / Keep practising, because this page is read by a ten-year-old. The parent-facing bands stay Secure / Developing / Needs work. If you want one vocabulary everywhere, say so and I'll switch it.
  • Threshold. Maths is greyed as "Not started". Settled: under 20 attempts in the last 30 days, no band. BAND_MIN_ATTEMPTS and BAND_WINDOW_DAYS in analytics/services.py.
  • Accuracy window. "The last month" is 30 days, BAND_WINDOW_DAYS, which is deliberately not WEEKLY_WINDOW_DAYS's 7.
  • Parent summary tab. Not drawn here - the panel behind it is the CTO's - but the tab is in the build, unchanged, and its markup is untouched. The grey canvas below is shared with it: its cards are still white, but the ground behind them is no longer a white-to-off-white gradient.
  • Homework "Late". The current page shows a 12-day-overdue task in a neutral "Assigned" pill. This is the only non-grey, non-accent colour on the page. Check that flagging lateness to the child rather than the parent is what you want.

Before this ships

Every band on this page is computed from Attempt. import_pack.py:301-303 deletes questions on (section, source) and Attempt.question is on_delete=CASCADE, so a deploy that re-imports a pack empties these rows and every subject falls back to "Not started". The layout is safe to build; the bands are not trustworthy until that is fixed.
