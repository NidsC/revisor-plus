### QR0002
> Picture Perfect is a photography company offering portraits of individuals, families, babies and pets. Extras (props, photoshop, custom backgrounds) cost more.


**1. (QR0002 Q1)** Debbie has 3 children. She wants two family portraits and an individual portrait of all of them (Debbie + 3 children). She wants all of the photographs photoshopped. How much does she pay in total including the bundle discount?

- £115.25
- £117.50
- £118.75
- £120.75  ✅
- £122.50

_4 individual x 1.15 x 20 = 92; 2 family x 1.15 x 30 = 69; total 161; 6 photos = 25% off; 161 x 0.75 = £120.75._


**2. (QR0002 Q2)** Bill wants portraits of his two dogs. He has paid for props, photoshop and custom backgrounds for each portrait. How much does this cost in total?

- £68.50
- £69.25
- £70.25  ✅
- £71.50
- £72.25

_1 dog with photoshop: 17.50 x 1.15 = 20.125; + props + background: 20.125 + 5 + 10 = 35.125; x 2 dogs = £70.25._


**3. (QR0002 Q3)** Bill is told that if he gets a portrait of himself as well he will get a 10% discount overall. He decides to get a basic portrait of himself with no additional features. How much more is his new total to the nearest pound?

- £10.00
- £11.00  ✅
- £12.00
- £13.00
- £14.00

_70.25 + 20 = 90.25; x 0.9 = 81.225; 81.225 - 70.25 = 10.975 ~= £11._


**4. (QR0002 Q4)** Laura and John have just had a baby. They want a portrait of the baby, an individual portrait of each of themselves and a family portrait. They opt for custom backgrounds and props but not photoshop. How much does this cost in total including the bundle discount?

- £130.25
- £131.75  ✅
- £132.50
- £133.75
- £134.25

_Baby 25+5+10; 2 individual 2x(20+5+10); family 30+5+10; total 155; 4 portraits = 15% off; 155 x 0.85 = £131.75._

### QR0025  `[chart]`

**14. (QR0025 Q1)** Which item sold the most across the year?

- Phone
- Tablet
- E-Reader

- All three sold the same number

**15. (QR0025 Q2)** The E-Reader sold for £25 a device in Quarters 1 to 3 but was discounted by 20% in Quarter 4. How much revenue was made from sales of this device across the year?

- £2500
- £3550
- £3600
- £3950  ✅
- £4000

_(100 x 25) + (25 x 25) + (25 x 25) + (20 x [25 x 0.8=20]) = 2500 + 625 + 625 + 200 = £3950._


**16. (QR0025 Q3)** The E-Reader sold 120 units in the period of January to May. How many units did it sell in June?

- 0
- 5  ✅
- 10
- 20
- 25

_Quarters 1 and 2 (Jan-Jun) total 125; 125 - 120 = 5._


**17. (QR0025 Q4)** When analyzing the sales of two sales employees Dan and Tom, the company found that in Quarters 1 and 2, Dan sold twice as many tablets as Tom. How many tablets did Tom sell in Quarters 1 and 2?

- 10
- 20  ✅
- 30
- 40
- 60

_Total tablet sales in Q1 and Q2 = 60; Dan sold twice as many as Tom, so Tom sold 60 x 1/3 = 20._


### QR0012
> A gym in central London offers monthly memberships and day passes. Joining fee £20.00 for monthly memberships (not day passes). Premium memberships include fitness classes; standard do not. Classes can be bought independently with no joining fee.


**18. (QR0012 Q1)** Jessica and Sarah are students looking to join the gym. Sarah wants a 20 class pass. Jessica only wants to join for 6 months: she buys a day pass and a standard student membership for 6 months. Including the joining fee, how much more expensive is Jessica's package than Sarah's, by percentage?

- 12%
- 13%
- 14%
- 15%  ✅
- 16%

_Sarah: 128. Jessica: 7 + 20 + 120 = 147. 147 - 128 = 19; 19 / 128 x 100 = 14.8% ~= 15%._


**19. (QR0012 Q2)** On the basis that there are on average 30 days in a month, how much cheaper is the daily rate of a six month premium membership compared to a single premium day pass?

- £7.00
- £8.00
- £9.00
- £10.00
- £11.00  ✅

_Six month premium daily rate: 180 / (6 x 30) = £1; premium day pass £12; 12 - 1 = £11._


**20. (QR0012 Q3)** How much cheaper is a student 20 classes pass per class compared with a standard 5 classes pass per class?

- £3.60  ✅
- £4.00
- £4.40
- £4.80
- £5.20

_Standard 5 pass per class: 50 / 5 = £10; student 20 pass per class: 128 / 20 = £6.40; 10 - 6.40 = £3.60._


**21. (QR0012 Q4)** Anthony is a student looking to join for 4 months. He is debating between 4 one-month passes at the premium student rate or a six month pass at the standard student rate. Which is cheaper and by how much?

- Four premium one month passes, by £8.00
- Four premium one month passes, by £12.00  ✅
- Four premium one month passes, by £16.00
- Standard six month pass, by £8.00
- Standard six month pass, by £12.00

_Four premium one-month passes + joining fee: (27 x 4) + 20 = 128; standard six month pass + joining fee: 120 + 20 = 140; 140 - 128 = £12 cheaper for the four premium passes._

### QR0003
> A salon offers services at prices depending on whether done by a senior, intermediate or junior professional. Students get 15% discount, loyalty customers 10%, loyalty students 20%. Every 5th week, wash and blow dry is half price.


**29. (QR0003 Q1)** Sandra is a loyalty customer. She wants full head highlights and a hair cut. The highlights will be done by a senior employee and the hair cut by an intermediate employee. How much does this cost in total?

- £59.00
- £60.00
- £61.00
- £62.00
- £63.00  ✅

_(40 + 30) x 0.9 = £63._


**30. (QR0003 Q2)** Frances is a student who wants a permanent colour, hair cut and a wash and blow dry. At first she wants it done by an intermediate employee but changes her mind and opts for a junior. How much cheaper is the total cost by percentage if she gets it done by a junior?

- 15%
- 18%
- 24%  ✅
- 28%
- 35%

_Intermediate (35+30+17.5) x 0.85 = 70.125; junior (25+22.5+15) x 0.85 = 53.125; (70.125-53.125)/70.125 = 24%._


**31. (QR0003 Q3)** Liz is both a student and a loyalty customer. She wants a hair cut and a wash and blow dry, done by a junior employee; she has had 4 wash and blow dries prior to this. How much more expensive would it be if she wasn't a student, not a loyalty customer, had only 3 wash and blow dries before, and got it done by a senior staff member?

- £41.00  ✅
- £49.75
- £54.01
- £57.64
- £64.12

_Liz (student+loyalty, junior, 5th wash so half price): (22.5 + 7.5) x 0.8 = 24; senior no discount, full price wash: 45 + 20 = 65; 65 - 24 = £41._


### QR0011
> An investment bank has three offices - London, New York and Hong Kong - trying to arrange a meeting suitable for all. When it is 13:00 in London on the 6th of October, it is 20:00 in Hong Kong and 8:00 in New York on the same day. The latest a meeting can go on until is 23:00 in any country, and the earliest one can start is 5:00.


**32. (QR0011 Q1)** How long is the window period in which all three offices can all have a meeting together?

- 1 hour
- 2 hours
- 3 hours
- 4 hours
- 5 hours  ✅

_Earliest start uses New York (furthest behind): 5:00 NY = 10:00 London = 18:00 Hong Kong. Latest end uses Hong Kong (furthest ahead) at 23:00. 23:00 - 18:00 = 5 hours._
