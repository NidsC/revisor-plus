Version 1.3 · Effective 21 November 2026 · Served at /privacy/ · Governing law: England and Wales

1. Who we are

RevisorPlus is an online practice service for the 11+ exams (English, Maths, Verbal Reasoning and Non-Verbal Reasoning), run from the United Kingdom.

Data controller: RevisorPlus, registered with the Information Commissioner's Office. All contact is by email: revisorplus@gmail.com.

Contact for anything in this policy: revisorplus@gmail.com

We do not have a Data Protection Officer; we are not required to appoint one. The contact address above is the right place for every question, request or complaint about personal data.

2. The short version, for pupils

RevisorPlus is a website you use to practise for your 11+ exam. Here is what it knows about you, in plain words:

  • The nickname your parent chose for you and the username you log in with. Not your full name, not your age, not your address, not the school you go to.
  • The school you are aiming for, the date of your exam, and the scores you are aiming for.
  • Which questions you answered, what you answered, and whether you got them right. We use this to work out what to practise next and to show you and your parent how you are doing.
  • If your parent has linked a tutor, the homework your tutor sets you and how you are doing on it.

We never show your practice to anyone except you, your parent and a tutor your parent has linked. We do not sell it, we do not advertise to you, and we do not track you around the internet. If you or your parent want us to delete everything about you, your parent can email us and we will.

4.4 Technical data

Our hosting provider keeps standard web server logs (IP address, browser type, the pages requested, timestamps) for security and fault-finding. We do not use these to track or profile anyone.

If you accept advertising cookies on our offer and home pages, Meta's pixel sets its own cookies in your browser and we send Meta a record of three actions: starting the sign-up form, paying for a first month, and the first renewal payment. That record includes a hashed form of your email address, your internet address and browser type, and the amount paid. It never includes anything about your child.

9. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us to:

  • See the data we hold about you or your child (a subject access request). We will reply within one month with a copy of everything, in a readable form, free of charge.
  • Correct anything inaccurate.
  • Delete your account, or just one pupil's account. Deleting a parent account deletes all pupils under it.
  • Restrict or object to processing in the circumstances the law allows.
  • Receive the data you gave us in a machine-readable form.
  • Complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113. We would rather you came to us first so we can put things right.

To use any of these rights, email revisorplus@gmail.com from the address on your account. There is no self-service deletion button yet; deletion is carried out by us, by hand, and we will confirm by email when it is done. Because a pupil cannot have an email address, requests about a pupil come from the parent; a pupil can also ask their parent to make a request, and we will treat a pupil's own message to us with the same care.

10. Children, and what we do differently for them

The ICO's Age Appropriate Design Code applies to RevisorPlus because pupils are children. In line with it:

  • Best interests. The pupil's practice data exists to help the pupil learn. We do not use it for anything else.
  • Minimisation. We do not ask a child's age, date of birth, location, address, current school or full name, and the system cannot store them.
  • No profiling beyond practice, no nudges, no advertising. There is nothing in the service designed to keep a child on it longer or to sell to them.
  • No advertising cookies on a child's pages. Our advertising measurement runs only on the public pages and the parent's sign-up and payment pages, never on any page a pupil uses.
  • Parental control. Only a parent can create a pupil account, set or change its password, link a tutor, subscribe or cancel, and request deletion. Parents can see everything the pupil does on the service.
  • Transparency. Section 2 explains the service in words a pupil can read.
  • Sharing. A pupil's data is visible only to the pupil, their parent, and a tutor the parent has linked. Tutors see progress and set homework; they cannot see a parent's billing details.
  • Sign-in with Google is for adults only. A pupil can never sign in with Google, and the system refuses to create a pupil account from a Google sign-in.

11. Security

Every page is served over HTTPS. Passwords are stored as salted one-way hashes. Card details never reach our servers. Access to the database is limited to the people who run the service. Card payments are handled by Stripe, which is certified to PCI DSS Level 1; our hosting provider publishes its own security certifications.

No online service can promise that a breach is impossible. If one happens and it is likely to put your rights at risk, we will tell you and the ICO without undue delay, as the law requires.

12. Cookies

We use two cookies the website needs to work: a session cookie that keeps you logged in and a security (CSRF) cookie that protects forms. They are strictly necessary and need no consent. On our home and offer pages we also ask whether you accept advertising cookies from Meta, which measure whether our adverts lead to sign-ups. They are set only if you choose Accept; Reject is just as easy, and you can change your choice on the Cookie choices page linked in the footer. Your choice is stored in a cookie for six months. We use no analytics cookies.

13. Changes to this policy

We will post any change here with a new version number and effective date. If a change affects what we collect about pupils or how long we keep it, we will email every parent before it takes effect.
